Straight answers.

Do I need to stop running ads?

No — if ads are working for you, keep running them. Our point is sequencing, not opposition: fix what converts the traffic you already get first, then ads go further with every dollar. We also build and run ads ourselves once that foundation is in place.

I already have a website. Do I need a new one?

Usually, yes — most schools' sites were built once and never touched again. We'll tell you honestly on the call if a rebuild isn't the highest-leverage move for you yet.

How long does it take to launch?

Most schools are live within a few weeks of kickoff, site and automation together — not a six-month agency timeline.

What's actually included?

The website, the review & referral automation, and the lead follow-up + past-member reactivation system — one build, one monthly rate, no separate tools to stitch together yourself.
